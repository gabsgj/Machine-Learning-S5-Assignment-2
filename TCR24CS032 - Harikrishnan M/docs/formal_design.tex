\section{System Overview}
This document specifies the formal design of the capability composition model. State transitions are governed by dense vector mappings.

\section{Vector Space Model}
Let $d$ be the dimensionality of the state space. Each capability $C$ is embedded in a vector space $\mathbb{R}^{2d+3}$:
\begin{equation}
\vec{v}_C = \begin{bmatrix} \vec{p} \\ \vec{e} \\ c \\ r \\ a \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{2d+3}
\end{equation}
where $\vec{p} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ represents preconditions, $\vec{e} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ represents effects, $c \in \mathbb{R}^+$ denotes cost, $r \in [0, 1]$ denotes reliability, and $a \in [0, 1]$ denotes availability.

\section{Composition Operators}
Given $C_1$ and $C_2$, the composition $C_{12} = C_2 \circ C_1$ is defined component-wise as:
\begin{align}
p_{12, i} &= \max(p_{1, i}, p_{2, i} - e_{1, i}) \quad \forall i \in \{1, \dots, d\} \\
e_{12, i} &= \max(e_{1, i}, e_{2, i}) \quad \forall i \in \{1, \dots, d\} \\
c_{12} &= c_1 + c_2 \\
r_{12} &= r_1 \times r_2 \\
a_{12} &= \min(a_1, a_2)
\end{align}
